```
From September 1944, the Focke-Wulf Dora-9 was a late-war Luftwaffe high-altitude interceptor. While it carried less armor and fewer guns than the heavily armed A-8, it achieved a significantly faster top speed, a better climb rate, and superior overall performance at high altitudes.
```
## **Pros:**
- Great roll capacities;
- High speed at low/medium altitude;
- Excellent acceleration and climb;
- [basic-about-maneuvers](basic-about-maneuvers.md)- use flat or rolling scissors; Boom-zoom;
## **Cons:**
- Awful night operation cockpit illumination;
- Poor maneuverability in low speed; 
- Poor dogfight performance under 2.000m or over 6.000m;
- External tank drag punish more D-9 than A-8.

## **1)** D-9 > **Cold start:**

1. ~~(If applicable) Turn your `personal flashlight` ON;~~
2. ~~(If applicable) Turn `Cockpit lights` ON;~~
3. Open `Canopy` (crew needs to listen to you);
4. Request `Start-Up` procedure to tower;
5. Check the current weather temperature in the `Briefing` screen;
6. (If applicable) Ask `Ground Crew` to "Rearm and Refuel", and wait for the `"Rearming"` and/or `"Refueling"` answer;
7. **Electric:**
	1. Ask `Ground Crew` for `Ground Electrical Power` <span style="color: orange;">ON</span>;
	2. (If applicable) Turn `Cockpit lights` <span style="color: orange;">ON</span>;
	3. Turn `Circuit Breakers` (right side, low panel) <span style="color: orange;">ON</span>:
		1. Mandatory (from top): <span style="color: orange;">All, except 3rd bt</span>;
		2. (If applicable):
			- If dark: --
			- If cold: <span style="color: orange;">3rd</span>; 
			- If raining: <span style="color: orange;">3rd</span>;
			- If dropping bombs: --
		3. In `Kraftstoffpumpen` (fuel pumps) (right side, lower panel):
			1. (If applicable) Including `External Fuel Tank`: <span style="color: orange;">All</span>;
			2. Otherwise, just the mandatory (from top to bottom): <span style="color: orange;">All, except E85</span>;
8. **Fuel:**
    1. In `Fuel Tank Selector` (front panel, red valve on the left):
        1. Put it fully up in <span style="color: orange;">Auf (On/Auto)</span>;
        2. (If applicable) Case `External Fuel Tank`, put that in <span style="color: orange;">Vorderer B...</span> pos;
	        1. Info: it closes the forward tank, making the `External Fuel Tank` refill only the rear tank while the rear tank feeds the engine.
    2. In `Fuel Gauge Selector` (right knee, black selector), set <span style="color: orange;">Hinten (rear)</span> tank to monitor that.
    3. (If applicable) In cold weather:
	    1. ~~`Primer Pump`~~ D9 doesn't need pump it at all on DCS, unfortunately;
    4. Make sure `MW-40` switch (left side, lower panel, rear of big red bt) is <span style="color: orange;">Aus (OFF, back)</span>;
    5. (If applicable) Extra fuel:
	    1. (If applicable) `MW 50 Boost` fluid was replaced by extra fuel, <span style="color: orange;">NEVER turn it "Ein" (ON, forward)</span>, once it would be catastrophically injected into the supercharger;
9. **Engine:**
    1. In `Magneto Switch`, select <span style="color: orange;">M1+M2</span> option (fully forward);
    2. `Unlock throttle` ("throttle to start" command), and keep it in <span style="color: orange;">idle</span> pos;
    3. <span style="color: orange;">Hold</span> both `Wheel Brakes` down OR ask crew <span style="color: orange;">to place</span> `Wheel Chocks`;
    4. In `Starter Switch` (right side, below `Canopy Hand Crank`):
        1. Open `FREI` protection;
        2. <span style="color: orange;">Press down and hold</span> the switch (left click), waiting <span style="color: orange;">25 secs</span>;
        3. After that, <span style="color: orange;">release it</span> and then <span style="color: orange;">pull it up</span> once (right click).
    5. Info: <span style="color: orange;">the engine should starts now!</span>
10. **Warm the engine up:**
    1. ~~Make sure the `Radiator Flaps` are fully CLOSED;~~
    2. Slide `Throttle` up until `Tachometer` hits <span style="color: orange;">1200 RPM</span>;
        - Keep it until `Oil temp.` gauge (behind `Stick`, orange one) hits <span style="color: orange;">40ºC</span>;
    3. Now, `Tachometer` to <span style="color: orange;">1800 RPM</span>;
    4. (If applicable) Scramble situation:
	    1. ==xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx==
    5. **Meanwhile:**
	    - Turn `Battery` button (`Circuit Breakers` panel, last one) <span style="color: orange;">ON (in)</span>;
	    - Ask `Ground Crew` to disconnect `Ground Electric Power`;
	    - ==Turn Radio (xxxxxxxx) ON and set this up;==
	    1. In `Artificial Horizon`, rotate it <span style="color: orange;">fully anti-clockwise</span>;
	    2. ==In `Altimeter` gauge, set it to zero!==
    6. Wait for `Oil temp.` in <span style="color: orange;">60ºC</span>;
    7. (Automatic system) once `Coolant Temp.` gauge (behind `Stick`, green monitor) indicates <span style="color: orange;">>=90ºC</span>, <span style="color: orange;">OPEN</span> `Radiator Flaps`;
    8. Once warmed up, slide back the `Throttle` to <span style="color: orange;">idle</span>.
    9. <span style="color: orange;">Release</span> `Wheel Brakes` OR <span style="color: orange;">remove</span> `Wheel Chocks`;
    10. Ready to go!

## **2)** D-9 > **Taxiing:**

1. Make sure `Nav. Lights` are <span style="color: orange;">ON</span>;
2. (If applicable) Case `External Fuel Tank`, make sure `Fuel Tank Selector` is in <span style="color: orange;">Vorderer B...</span> pos;
3. Make sure `Canopy` is <span style="color: orange;">open</span>;
4. Keep in mind: once `Coolant Temp.` gauge (behind `Stick`, green monitor) indicates <span style="color: orange;">>=90ºC</span>, <span style="color: orange;">OPEN</span> `Radiator Flaps`;
5. Say on radio: `YourCallsign` taxiing!
6. To move, `Throttle` (<span style="color: orange;">1200-1400 RPM</span>) and `Wheel Brakes` gently;
7. If needed, pull the `Stick` <span style="color: orange;">back to lock</span> `Tail Wheel`;
8. (If applicable) Request to take off and wait for your go;

## 3) D-9 > Takeoff:
EXTRA TIP: Check where you are on the map (or on the `kneeboard`);

1. **Preparing:**
    1. Make sure `Nav. Lights` are <span style="color: orange;">ON</span>;
    2. (If applicable) Case `External Fuel Tank`, make sure `Fuel Tank Selector` is in <span style="color: orange;">Vorderer B...</span> pos;
    3. In `Fuel Gauge Selector` set <span style="color: orange;">Hinten (rear)</span> tank to monitor that;
    4. On the runway, you are already in takeoff pos;
    5. `Canopy` must be <span style="color: orange;">closed</span>;
    6. (Automatic system) `Radiator Flaps` should be <span style="color: orange;">at least a bit OPEN</span>;
    7. `Flaps` to <span style="color: orange;">takeoff</span> pos (left side, lower panel, center yellow button);
    8. Based in your weight:
        1. Light:
            - Make sure `MW 50 Boost` switch (left lower panel, near to `Magnetos`) is <span style="color: orange;">OFF (back)</span>.
            - Trim nose (stabilizer) up to <span style="color: orange;">+1.0</span> (left side, weird right wheel);
        2. Heavy:
            - (If applicable) If MW 50 is not for extra fuel, turn `MW 50 Boost` switch <span style="color: orange;">ON (forward)</span>;
            - Trim nose (stabilizer) up to <span style="color: orange;">+2.0</span> (left side, weird right wheel);
    9. Make sure everything from "MEANWHILE" Cold-start procedures was done;
2. **Go:**
    1. Say on radio: `YourCallsign` rolling!
    2. Pull the `Stick` <span style="color: orange;">back</span> (it locks the `Tail Wheel`), slide `Throttle` forward slowly until the throttle's rail <span style="color: orange;">Start</span> pos;
    3. <span style="color: orange;">Release</span> the `Stick` slowly, and, in <span style="color: orange;">180kmh</span>, pull it back again;
3. **Once airborne:**
    - `Gears` <span style="color: orange;">UP</span> (unlock and press the right-RED-button) - limit speed's 250kmh;
    - `Flaps` <span style="color: orange;">UP</span> to 0º (left-RED-button) - limit speed's 290kmh;
    - Say on radio: `YourCallsign` airborne!
    - Trim adjustments!
    - Turn `Oxygen` valve (right side, low panel, blue one) <span style="color: orange;">ON (up)</span>;
    - (If applicable and not fuel in) Turn `MW 50 Boost` switch (near to `Magnetos`) <span style="color: orange;">ON (forward)</span>;
    1. **(If applicable) In combat skies:**
        - Turn `Nav. Lights` <span style="color: orange;">OFF</span>;
        - ~~Turn `Cabin illumination` OFF;~~
        - Turn `Master Arm` switch to <span style="color: orange;">ON (up)</span>;

## 6) D-9 > Special Ops:

- **(If) Discarding `External Fuel Tank`:**
	- Info:  Once the <span style="color: orange;">rear (hinten)</span> internal tank's fuel level begins to get lower than <span style="color: orange;">240L</span> it means the external tank is dry.
	- In `Fuel Tank Selector`, put it in the <span style="color: orange;">Auf (On/Auto)</span> pos;
	- In `Kraftstoffpumpen` (Fuel Circuit Breakers) panel, turn the <span style="color: orange;">E85 button OFF</span>;
	- Pull `Rumpflast` handle (left knee, lower red handle) to release the external tank;
- **(If) Firing rockets:**
	- In `Main Rocket Switch` (front panel, behind `Stick`, left bt) turn it <span style="color: orange;">ON (up)</span>;
	- Firing:
		- (If R4M, 55mm) Trigger ones to fire one couple of rockets at time;
		- (If BR21, 210mm) Trigger ones to fire one rocket at time;
	- To discard the rockets, use `Rocket Emergency Release` in bombs panel;

- **For Engine:**
    - **Failure:**

        - (If applicable) Jettison all external load;
        - Read section: 8) Landing > Emergency landing;
    - **On fire:**
        - Slide `Throttle` fully back to <span style="color: orange;">idle</span> pos;

## 8) D-9 > **Landing:**

1. **Before:**
    1. (if applicable) `External fuel Tank` must be <span style="color: orange;">jettison</span>;
    2. `Nav. Lights` <span style="color: orange;">ON</span>;
    3. ==Set `Trim` to -2 to -3 (nose up);==
    4. ==Vel. 300Km/h (`ATA` 1.0), 200m alt;==
    5. (If applicable) Emergency landing:
        1. Nothing to do for now!
    6. Turn `Master Arm` switch to <span style="color: orange;">OFF (down)</span>;
2. **Approaching/Landing:**
    1. `Flaps` landing (green left bt) - limit speed's ==300Km==/h;
    2. `Gears` down (green right bt) - limit speed's ==XXXKm==/h;
    3. ==Vel. 250Km/h (`ATA` ~0.7);==
    4. ==Alt. 200-100m;==
    5. (If applicable) Emergency landing:
        - (If) Don't use `Gears` for soft terrain/water;
        - Set `Fuel Tank Selector` <span style="color: orange;">fully down to Zu (close)</span>;
        - Pull `Magneto` <span style="color: orange;">fully back</span>;
        - In `Kill Switch` <span style="color: orange;">press it</span>;
        - `Canopy` must be <span style="color: orange;">jettison</span>;
    6. ==Once on the ground (180-160Km/h), `Throttle` in idle, and use the `Wheel Brakes` carefully;==
    7. `Stick` back to lock the `Tail Wheel` until taxi speed;
3. **After regular landing:**
    1. `Stick` must be <span style="color: orange;">released</span> to unlock `Tail Wheel`;
    2. Leave any runway;
    3. `Canopy` must be <span style="color: orange;">open</span>;
    4. `Flaps` <span style="color: orange;">up</span>;
    5. Taxi it;

## **10)** D-9 > **Turn it off:**

1. Make sure you have `Canopy` open;
2. Ask crew to place `Wheel Chocks`;
3. **Engine:**
    1. ==Slide down the `Throttle` until 12 (1200 RPM) for 2 min to lower cylinder head temperatures;==
    2. **Meanwhile:**
        - Turn `Oxygen` valve OFF (down);
        - Turn `Master Arms` switch OFF (down);
        - ==(If applicable) Turn `Main Rocket` switch OFF (down);==
        - ==(If applicable) Turn `Bomb Fusing Selector` to AUS (up-center) pos;==
    3. After that time, cut `Throttle` to idle;
    4. Set `Magneto Switch` to 0 (full back);
    5. `Lock Throttle` ("throttle to stop" command);
    6. `Radiator Flaps` must be <span style="color: orange;">fully closed</span>;
4. **Fuel:**
    1. In `Fuel Tank Selector`, put it down in `ZU` (close);
5. **Electric:**
    1. Press `Kill Switch` button (left knee, lower panel, big red one);
    2. ==Turn all `Circuit Breakers` OFF, including `Kraftstoffpumpen` and `Schusswaffen._==
6. Done!
