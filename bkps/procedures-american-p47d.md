## **1) P47 > Cold start:**

1. (Maybe) Turn your `personal flashlight` ON;
2. Open `Canopy` (crew needs to listen to you);
3. Request `Start-Up` procedure to tower;
4. Check the current weather temperature in the `Briefing` screen;
5. (Maybe) Ask `Ground Crew` to "Rearm and Refuel", and wait for the `"Rearming"` and/or `"Refueling"` answer;
6. **Electric:**
    1. Ask `Ground Crew` for `Ground Electrical Power` ON;
    2. (Maybe) Turn `Cockpit lights` ON;
    3. In `Circuit Breakers` (left side, lower black panel):
	    1. Turn `Generator Switch` (lowest left panel side) ON (up);
    4. ==(Maybe):==
        1. ==Heavy duty operation weight: ask for the crew to `Ground Electric Power`;==
        2. ==If cold/high-alt.: Turn `Windscreen De-Icer` knob (right leg) to ON (upper red end pointing to the back);==
7. **Fuel:**
    1. ==(Maybe) Ask `Ground Crew` to add the `External Fuel Tank`;==
    2. Set `Main Fuel Selector Valve` (left side, below the red ball lever) to `Main On` (forward) pos with the smallest side of the valve;
    3. In `Primer` valve (front panel, right side, a elliptical valve):
	    1. Unlock it (right click)
	    2. Pump it (left click) 5x;
	    3. Lock it again;
    4. Slide `Throttle` forward just a bit (10%) to make the fuel flow;
    5. ==(Maybe) Using an `External Fuel Tank`:==
        1. ==Pull `Drop Tank` lever (right leg) to ON (back);==
        2. ==Turn `Main Tank` lever to OFF (down);==
8. **Engine:**
    1. In`Air Filter Control` lever (left side, on floor), active it, pulling it UP;
    2. ==In `Supercharger Mode Auto` switch (right below the `Tachometer`), set to AUTO (down);==
    3. In `Magnetos` (front panel, left side, red lever), set it in `Both` pos (fully right);
    4.  In Throttle Switch panel (left side, `Throttle` upper corner):
	    1. In `Oil Cooler Shutter Switch`, press and hold (using left click) until its marker (on the left of switch) be over the `Neutral` pos;
	    2. In `Intercooler Shutter Switch`, press and hold (using right click) until its marker be over the `Neutral` pos;
    5. Slide `Pitch` lever (black one side by side with `Throttle`) fully forward;
    6. In `Parking Brake Handle` lever (behind the `Stick`), pulling it (right click) and press both `Brake Pedals`. First, release the `Brake Pedals` and, after that, the `Parking` lever;
    7. Starting final step:
	    1. In `Starter Switch` (front panel, right side, below `Tachometer` gauge):
		    1. Select `Engage` pos (right click) just once;
		    2. Press and hold `Energize` pos (left click) for 15 secs and than release it;
		    3. Press and hold `Engage` pos (right click) and go to the next step holding it;
	    2. Slide `Mixture Lever` (red "M" lever with `Throttle`) to `Auto Rich` pos;
	    3. Once `Tachometer` reaches 800 RPM, release the `Starter Switch`;
    8. THE ENGINE SHOULD START NOW!
9. **Warm the engine up:**
    1. Using `Throttle`, set `Tachometer` needle to 1200 RPM;
    2. (Maybe) Scramble situation:
	    1. Press and hold `Cowl Flaps Control` knob (front-right side, below `Primer`) until the engine flaps are totally closed (it will dangerously increase temperatures);
    3. **Meanwhile:**
        - Turn `Battery Switch` (front panel, left side, over the red valve) ON (left);
        - Ask `Ground Crew` to disconnect `Ground Electric Power`;
        - ==Turn Radio (xxxxxxxx) ON and set this up;==
        - Unlock `Artificial Horizon`, using its big `Cage` knob, twisting it to ON pos;
        - In `Altimeter` gauge, set it to zero!
        - In `Directional Gyro` gauge (front panel, left side), set it to the same value as the `North Magnetic` shown by that front panel centered gauge;
        - Make sure `Tail Wheel lock` lever (right side, floor) is unlocked (back/right pos);
    4. Waiting for:
	    1. `Cylinder Temp` gauge (right side of `Starter Switch`) to reach 100 LBS;
	    2. `Oil Temp` gauge (over `Cylinder Temp` one) to reach 40ºC;
	    3. `Oil Pressure` gauge stabilizes between 70-90 LBS;
    5. Once warmed up, slide `Throttle` back to idle;
    6. Release the `Wheel Brakes` pressing the pedals once;
10. Info: ready to go!

## **2) P47 > Taxiing:**

1. Make sure `Nav. Lights` are ON;
2. Make sure `Canopy` is open;
3. Release Boost lever's `Interconnection Latch` (behind/attached with `Throttle`), keeping `Boost lever` in idle;
4. Make sure `Pitch` is fully forward (up);
5. Make sure `Air Filter Control` lever is activated (UP);
6. Say on radio: `YourCallsign` taxiing!
7. To move the bird, use only `Throttle` at ==11 (1100 RPM)==, and `Wheel Brakes` gently. 
8. (Maybe) Request to take off and wait for your go;

## **3) P47 > Takeoff:**
EXTRA TIP: Check where you are on the map (or on the `kneeboard`);

1. **Preparing:**
    - Make sure `Nav. Lights` are ON;
    - On the runway, you are already in takeoff position;
    - Close the `Canopy` (USAF doctrine if not carrier-based airplane);
    - Lock `Tail Wheel` (right side, on floor) moving it forward;
    - Make sure `Pitch` is fully forward (up);
    - Make sure Boost lever's `Interconnection Latch` is not attached to `Throttle` and still in idle;
    - Unlock `Guns Safety Switch` (red cover behind `Throttle`);
    - Press and hold `Cowl Flaps Control` knob (front-right side, below `Primer`) until the engine flaps are almost closed (avoiding additional takeoff drag);
    -  Make sure `Air Filter Control` lever is activated (UP);
    - ==Make sure `Supercharger Mode Auto` switch is AUTO (down);==
    - `Trim`:
        - ==`Trim Nose` UP until its gauge hand touches the second stripe (from top);==
        - ==`Trim Rudder` (left thigh) all the way to the forward/right;==
    - `Flaps`:
        - If only guns and full internal fuel tank or less: no `flaps` needed;
        - If heavier (bombs and/or loaded w/ `External Fuel Tank`), move `Flaps Control Handle` (left side, orange one) back until `10 black mark` on flaps surface . When the `flaps` are down, over each wing, you can see a small tab indicator up;
    - Make sure everything from "MEANWHILE" Cold-start procedures was done;
2. **Go:**
    1. Say on radio: `YourCallsign` rolling!
    2. With `Stick` idle, slide `Throttle` fully forward slowly;
        - **If normal weight:**
            - When the speed hits 100mph, gently move `Stick` forward;
            - At 110 knots, pull `Stick` back to fly;
            - Don't climb much before 150mph;
        1. **If heavy weight:**
            - ==xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx==
            - ==xxxxxxxxxxxxx==
3. **Once airborne:**
    - `Gears up` - limit speed's ==160mph;==
    - (Maybe) `Flaps up` to 0º - limit speed's ==140mph;==
    - Say on radio: `YourCallsign` airborne!
    - Fully close `Cowl Flaps`;
    - In`Air Filter Control` lever, deactivate it, pushing it DOWN (back/left);
    - In `Main Fuel Selector Valve`:
	    - Change to `Auxiliary On` pos (right);
	    - (Maybe) For `External Tank`, change to `External On` pos (left) instead;
    - ==In `Oxygen` valve (left side, black wing knob), open it (vertical) to arm the auto feed (idle until 10K feet);==
    - Trim adjustments!
    1. **(Maybe) In combat skies:**
        - Turn `Nav. Lights` OFF;
        - ==Turn `Cabin illumination OFF`;==
        - ==Turn `Gunsight Master` switch (behind the gun sight, to the left) ON;==
        - ==In `Stick`, press `Safety Guns Pin` OUT (off)!==

## **4) P47 > Climbing & Cruise:**

Throttle / supercharger management. It's easy: Always use the throttle first, and when it's maxed out and you need more power, start moving the supercharger forward. When the overspeed light comes on, you've gone too far, move it back a bit.

- **Climbing:**
    - ==Ceiling: 43.000ft (~13km);==
    - ==`Pitch` 2850 RPM, `Throttle` +12 Boost (nose up to 170mph stable);==
    - ==From 18.000ft alt:==
        - ==Make sure `Circuit Breaker Pitot Heating` switch is ON (down);==
        - ==Turn `Windscreen De-Icer` knob (right leg) to ON (upper red end pointing to the back);==
    - ==From 25.000ft alt:==
        - ==Set `Fuel Tank Pressure` valve to ON (right);==
- **Cruise:**
    - ==(Maybe) Make sure the `External Fuel Tank` is being used;==
    - ==Safe cruise MAX = `Pitch` 2650 RPM, `Throttle` +7 Boost (Fuel = 80 igal/h);==
    - ==To save fuel = `Pitch` 1800 RPM, `Throttle +2` Boost (Fuel = 30 igal/h);==
    - ==Make sure `Radiators` switch is Auto (up);==

## 7) **P47 >** Failure Actions:

- **For Fuel leaking:**
    1. ==Pull `Mixture Control Select` lever full back to stop fuel flow;==
    2. ==Turn `Circuit Breaker Fuel Pump` OFF (up);==
    3. ==(Maybe) Keep the `External Fuel Tank` until you figure out where the leak is;==
        1. ==If fueling from `External Fuel Tank`, return to the Main Fuel Tank and jettison the external tank;==
        2. ==Open `Mixture Control Select` lever again;==
        3. ==Turn `Circuit Breaker Fuel Pump` ON (down);==
        4. ==Check the `Fuel` gauge. If not leaking anymore, restart the engine!==
- **For Fuel Low Pressure:**
    1. ==Set `Fuel Tank Pressure` to ON (right);==
- **For Engine on fire:**
    1. ==Pull `Mixture Control Select` lever full back to stop fuel flow;==
    2. ==Set `Supercharger Mode Auto` switch to `M.S.` (up);==
    3. ==Turn `Circuit Breaker Fuel Pump` OFF (up);==
    4. ==In `Radiators Flaps Auto` switch, set to ON (down);==
- **For Electrical issues:**
    1. ==In `Radiators Flaps Auto` switch, set to ON (down);==
- **For Radiators damage:**
    1. ==In `Radiators Flaps Auto` switch, set to AUTO/OFF (up);==

## **8) P47 > Landing:**

1. **Before:**
    1. (Maybe) Release `External Fuel Tank`;
    2. ==Set `Trim` to Nose UP;==
    3. ==`Nav. Lights` ON;==
    4. ==Make sure `Pitch` lever is in full forward position;==
    5. ==Set `Fuel Tank Pressure` valve to OFF (left);==
    6. ==Vel. 160mph, alt. 1000 feet;==
    7. (Maybe) Emergency landing:
        1. ==Jettison the `Canopy` with its red lever (over your head);==
        2. ==Set `Supercharger Mode Auto` switch to `M.S.` (up);==
        3. ==Turn `Circuit Breaker Fuel Pump` OFF (up);==
        4. ==(Maybe) Don't use Gear Down for soft terrain/water;==
    8. ==In `Stick`, press `Safety Guns Pin` IN (on)!==
    9.  In`Air Filter Control` lever (left side, on floor), active it, pulling it UP;
2. **Approaching/Landing:**
    1. ==`Flaps` down - limit speed's 140mph;==
    2. ==`Gears` down - limit speed's 160mph;==
    3. ==Vel. 110-100mph (RPM ~XX);==
    4. ==Alt. 650-350 feet;==
    5. (Maybe) Emergency landing:
        - ==Set `Magneto` switches OFF (down);==
        - ==In `Main Tank` lever, set to OFF (down);==
        - ==Pull `Mixture Control Select` lever full back;==
    6. ==Once on the ground (70-65mph), `Throttle` in idle, and use `Wheel Brakes` carefully;==
    7. ==When `Tail Wheel` touches the ground, pull the `Stick` back to lock it;==
3. **After regular landing:**
    1. ==Release the `Stick` to unlock the `Tail Wheel`;==
    2. Leave any runway;
    3. `Flaps` up;
    4. Turn `Circuit Breaker Pitot Heating` switch OFF (up);
    5. Taxi it;

## **9) P47 > Turn it off:**

1. ==Make sure the `Canopy` is open;==
2. ==Set the `Wheel Brakes`;==
3. **Engine:**
    1. ==Cool the engine for 2min at 800 RPM;==
        1. **Meanwhile:**
            1. ==Make sure `Safety Guns Pin` is IN (on);==
            2. ==Turn `Oxygen` knob OFF (horizontal pos);==
    2. ==Pull the `Pitch` lever full back;==
    3. ==Once the blades have stopped completely, turn `Magnetos` OFF;==
4. **Fuel:**
    1. ==Turn `Fuel Tank Pressure` valve OFF (left);==
    2. ==Pull `Mixture Control Select` lever full back;==
    3. ==In `Main Tank` lever, set it OFF (down);==
5. **Electric:**
    1. ==Hidden by the `Throttle`, turn the `Undercarriage Indicator Switch` OFF (down);==
    2. ==Turn OFF all `Circuit Breakers` (up)`, Gunsight, etc`;==
6. Done!
