## **Guns:**

- **Round types:**
    - API ........... Armor-Piercing Incendiary;
    - APT .......... Armor-Piercing Tracer;
    - HE ............ High-Explosive;
    - HEI-T ....... High-Explosive Incendiary Tracer;
    - Mine ........ Mine-Shell (Stronger High-Explosive);
    - I ................. Incendiary;
    - I-T ............. Incendiary Tracer;
- **2x Nose MG 131 (13mm), 475 rounds:**
    - Combat Mix (HE, HEI-T, API, APT)
    - Anti-Bomber (I, I-T, API, APT, HE, HEI-T)
    - Ground Attack (I, I-T, API, APT)
    - No tracers (HE, API)
    - Target Practice (API, APT, I-T)
- **2x Cowl Cannon 151/20E (20mm), 250 rounds:**
    - Combat Mix (Mine, HEI-T, API)
    - Anti-Bomber (Mine, HEI-T)
    - Ground Attack (API, HEI-T)
    - No tracers (API, Mine)
    - Target Practice (API, HEI-T)

## **Fuel & Range:**
Important: ranges are based on "Theoretical maximum"!

- **Internal tank (front + rear):**
    - Total capacity: 524L (138 gal);
    - Cruise range: ~835Km (~518mi);
- **Internal tank + aux internal tank:**
    - Aux tank: 115L (30 gal);
    - Total capacity: 639L (168 gal);
    - Cruise range: ~1.000Km (~621mi)
- **Internal tank + aux external tank:**
    - Aux tank: 300L (79 gal);
    - Total capacity: 824L (217 gal);
    - Cruise range: ~1.300Km (~807mi) - External tank drag punish more D-9 than A-8.
- **Internal tank + aux internal tank + aux external tank:**
    - Total capacity: 939L (248 gal);
    - Cruise range: ~1.450Km (~900mi);
